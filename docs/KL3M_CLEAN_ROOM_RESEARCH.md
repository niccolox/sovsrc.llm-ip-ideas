# KL3M research for independent model development

**Research date: 11 October 2026.** KL3M is a candidate for Sovereign Source's work on documented training permissions and creator rewards. Its developers emphasize source provenance, and its downloadable weights permit commercial adaptation. Those properties support evaluation as a licensed foundation or training component; they do not establish a legal clean-room defense for a downstream model.

**Recommendation:** evaluate `alea-institute/kl3m-003-3.7b` and the separate KL3M Data Project in a bounded provenance pilot. Keep the model, dataset, tokenizer and certification scopes distinct. KL3M is a research candidate alongside the selected Nemotron 3 Ultra target, not an adopted replacement. Frontier capability and a complete absence of Chinese model lineage are not established by this review.

## Model identity and US connection

273 Ventures developed KL3M and donated model and data assets to ALEA Institute in 2024. ALEA is incorporated in Michigan and publishes its nonprofit and tax records. This establishes a US organizational connection, not the geographic origin of every algorithm, dependency or training source. [273 Ventures history](https://273ventures.com/about/), [ALEA transparency records](https://aleainstitute.ai/transparency/).

| Artifact | Relevant facts |
| --- | --- |
| Reviewed checkpoint | `alea-institute/kl3m-003-3.7b` |
| Size and architecture | 3.69 billion parameters; Mixtral architecture with four experts and two selected per token. |
| Context and tokenizer | Model card specifies 4,096 tokens and the `kl3m-001-32k` tokenizer. |
| Training period | February–March 2024, according to the card. |
| Weights license | CC BY 4.0. |
| Intended role | Legal, regulatory and financial tasks; fine-tuning and use in training larger models. |

These are checkpoint-specific disclosures from the [model card](https://huggingface.co/alea-institute/kl3m-003-3.7b). It describes limited instruction alignment, so a conversational or tool-using SIP assistant would require separate evaluation and adaptation.

The older project website advertises an 8,192-token context for a planned 3.7B release, whereas this checkpoint's card specifies 4,096. Use the exact downloaded configuration and runtime tests to settle deployment limits. Historical launch plans are not evidence that a later model or feature shipped. [Project website](https://kl3m.ai/), [checkpoint documentation](https://huggingface.co/alea-institute/kl3m-003-3.7b).

## Training provenance and lineage

The checkpoint's developers describe its corpus as a US-only subset of the Kelvin Legal DataPack that they believe is entirely public domain. The card also says some original collection and training software was not donated. Full reproduction therefore requires more evidence than the released weights and newer datasets. [Model card](https://huggingface.co/alea-institute/kl3m-003-3.7b#training).

The project website says its larger models were trained from scratch using a Mixtral architecture and that KL3M excludes synthetic training data generated by other LLMs. These are developer disclosures. An architecture name does not establish inheritance of another model's weights, and the family-level statement should be confirmed against the exact checkpoint's records. [Training disclosures](https://kl3m.ai/).

For the project's earlier no-Qwen or no-Chinese-model condition, distinguish pretrained weights, teacher-generated examples, data-quality classifiers, tokenizers, code and research influences. A US maintainer or a from-scratch claim alone does not answer all of those questions. Require checkpoint-specific evidence before asserting that the condition is met, with a recorded definition of the relationships the ancestry screen excludes.

## The separate KL3M Data Project

The April 2025 research paper describes a later corpus and processing pipeline, with original documents, provenance metadata, extracted text and training-ready representations. Its admission protocol examines whether material was unprotected at creation, entered the public domain, or carries sufficient licensed permissions. The authors also consider commercial use, adaptations, attribution and collection restrictions. [KL3M Data Project paper, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07854v1).

The paper reports more than 132 million documents across 16 sources and a curated 579.8-billion-token snapshot. These are dated dataset figures, not the token count consumed by the reviewed 2024 checkpoint. The paper's legal interpretations are the authors' analysis, not court determinations or warranties. [Paper, contributions and protocol](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.07854v1).

For SIP admission, retain the original source and the basis for its classification. A document's availability on a government website should not be the sole reason for admitting every passage, attachment or third-party contribution. Distinguish rights in a collection from rights in its individual contents and review the intended jurisdiction and use. This is the proposed project admission standard; it does not adopt every source classification in the paper without review.

## What certification establishes

Fairly Trained lists KL3M under its **Licensed Model certification**. The public directory identifies the family but does not enumerate the precise checkpoint hashes covered. Obtain the applicable scope before describing this checkpoint or a new adaptation as certified. [Certification directory](https://www.fairlytrained.org/certified-models).

The current certification criteria accept training material provided under an appropriate agreement, appropriately open-licensed material, material in the public domain globally, or material fully owned by the developer. Third-party models and synthetic data must meet the same requirements. The program also requires due diligence and training-data records, reviews written submissions and reevaluates certification annually. [Certification requirements](https://www.fairlytrained.org/certifications).

This is evidence about a provider's training practices. It is not a court ruling, an indemnity, a guarantee that every output is noninfringing, or certification of Sovereign Source. The distinction between the card's US public-domain assessment and the certification program's global public-domain criterion is a concrete point to resolve when requesting the certification scope and supporting records.

## Commercial rights and attribution obligations

The checkpoint's CC BY 4.0 license permits reproduction, sharing and adaptations, including commercial activity. It contains no prohibition on building a competing model. When sharing covered material, including modified versions, preserve the required credits and notices, identify modifications and link the license. These are rights granted by the licensor within the license's scope; they do not independently clear everyone else's rights. [CC BY 4.0 legal text, sections 2–3](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).

CC BY 4.0 does not grant patent or trademark rights and disclaims warranties, including noninfringement. It also limits restrictions imposed on recipients' exercise of the licensed rights. Accordingly, the SIP license and optional marketplace agreements must preserve the applicable upstream freedoms. They cannot convert freely licensed KL3M material into a mandatory royalty entitlement. [License, sections 2 and 5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en).

Treat these permissions separately:

| Material or action | Review needed |
| --- | --- |
| Adapt or distribute the downloaded weights | Preserve applicable CC BY credits, notices and modification records. |
| Use tokenizer or training code | Review each artifact's own license and dependencies. |
| Generate examples for another model | Review input rights, output content and any applicable hosting agreement. |
| Import the newer dataset | Review the exact release, source records and associated obligations. |
| Reward new contributors | Use accepted agreements defining eligible use, beneficiaries and payment rules. |

The weight license does not assign ownership of every generated output or establish its complete source history. Separately hosted services may have additional terms; this research concerns the downloadable checkpoint.

## Clean room claims and independent development

US copyright law recognizes independent creation as a defense to copying. A clean-room process can provide evidence of independence, but calling a model clean does not establish that defense. [Eleventh Circuit civil jury instructions, section 9.24](https://www.ca11.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/courtdocs/clk/FormCivilPatternJuryInstructionsCurrentComplete.pdf).

| Development route | Accurate description and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Fine-tune KL3M weights | A licensed adaptation with disclosed ancestry; not independent pretraining. |
| Train separate weights on KL3M-generated examples | Synthetic-data training with a recorded teacher dependency; not automatic independent creation. |
| Train new weights on an independently reviewed corpus | Greater control over source admission, with evidence needed for each included source and development input. |

Passing a work through a model does not automatically remove its copyright implications. The Copyright Office's training report discusses rewriting and synthetic augmentation as potentially implicating derivative-work rights. It also describes fair use as fact-specific. That report is policy analysis, not a blanket judicial determination about KL3M or downstream models. [Copyright Office training report, pages 26 and 32](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-3-Generative-AI-Training-Report-Pre-Publication-Version.pdf).

A defensible public statement should identify the actual practices: documented source admission, retained provenance, permitted model use and agreed contributor payments. Claims of complete independence, universal clearance or legal immunity require substantially more evidence. Have IP counsel review the process and records before relying on a clean-room defense. The [independent model development pathway](CHATGPT_TO_INDEPENDENT_LLM_PATHWAY.md) remains applicable to any earlier restricted-service contributions.

## Proposed Sovereign Source pilot

Evaluate KL3M in one narrow domain before committing to broader training. Legal and regulatory source processing is a reasonable first experiment; scientific or mathematical reasoning needs its own capability evaluation. The following workflow is proposed work within the existing [solution architecture](SOLUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md):

1. **Register the artifacts.** Pin weights, tokenizer and code to immutable revisions; record hashes, licenses, notices, source dates and the governing service terms if hosted.
2. **Admit a bounded corpus.** Review a small set of original, licensed or public-domain documents and preserve paragraph-level identifiers and rights evidence.
3. **Record transformations.** For each generated example, retain source IDs, prompt, model revision, generation settings, reviewer decision and detected reproductions. A teacher's claimed citation is not sufficient evidence.
4. **Evaluate before adaptation.** Compare the unmodified model with the proposed adaptation on held-out tasks, factual support, citation accuracy, unintended reproduction and operating cost. Record failures and keep evaluation material out of training.
5. **Test rewards independently.** Use synthetic parties and simulated payouts to verify that accepted contribution use produces the agreed ledger entries without duplicates.

Rust/Axum should enforce permissions and reward eligibility; PostgreSQL should store the authoritative source and accounting records. Python validation and Prefect workflows can prepare approved manifests and supervise experiments. The model may propose attribution links, but it should not decide ownership or allocate money.

SIP can recognize ideas and influences through its voluntary contribution agreements. Copyright generally protects expression rather than the underlying idea, fact or method; broader recognition therefore needs a clearly stated contractual basis. [Copyright Office guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-protect.html). Preserve credit for known upstream sources without treating a similarity score as proof of ownership or a causal share of model behavior.

## Decision and evidence required

KL3M merits a provenance-focused pilot. Its value to this project is the combination of explicit source claims, a permissive commercial adaptation license and an external training-practices certification. It has not been established here as a frontier-capability teacher or as a fully reproducible foundation whose every upstream component satisfies the project's ancestry requirements.

Before adoption, assemble the exact training manifest and source classifications, checkpoint-specific lineage, certification scope, tokenizer and code licenses, and evidence for any synthetic-data or filtering dependencies. Resolve the missing original infrastructure records with ALEA or 273 Ventures. Preserve uncertainties explicitly rather than substituting the newer dataset for the old model's history.

The pilot should proceed to broader use only when the selected task passes documented capability criteria, the proposed uses are authorized, and required attribution and rewards survive the complete data flow. No model installation, training run or runtime benchmark accompanies this research document.
