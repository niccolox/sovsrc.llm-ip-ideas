# Ten leading data brokers and suppliers to frontier AI labs

**Project:** Super Sovereign Source LLM IP

**Research date:** October 14, 2026

**Purpose:** Compare commercial routes for buying permissioned training data and selling syndicate contributions to AI developers.

**Sovereign Source should evaluate Created by Humans for author-controlled books, Defined.ai for a broader dataset marketplace, Protege for negotiated proprietary content, and Surge AI for commissioned expert work.** These are different purchases. No single supplier in this comparison establishes the complete combination of source permission, individual attribution, downstream influence recognition and continuing contributor rewards required by SIP.

The ten-company shortlist covers six content licensing intermediaries and four expert-data suppliers. “Top” means selected for documented frontier relevance, a substantial commercial offering, or a useful rights-and-rewards model. It is not a revenue or market-share ranking: comparable audited figures and complete lab procurement lists are unavailable. Numbers identify entries rather than rank performance. Public offerings and dated relationships are distinguished below; prospective procurement still requires the actual agreement.

This report extends the [upstream suppliers and communities research](UPSTREAM_AI_DATA_SUPPLIERS_RESEARCH.md). The companion [Distillation as a Service report](DISTILLATION_AS_A_SERVICE_MARKET_RESEARCH.md) examines how licensed sources and permitted teacher outputs can become smaller deployable models.

## How the industry works

The supply chain has three commercial layers:

1. **Content licensing:** an intermediary aggregates existing books, recordings, images or business records and negotiates defined uses with model builders.
2. **Expert production:** a vendor recruits specialists to create demonstrations, solutions, rankings, evaluations and simulated work environments. The purchased input is newly produced labor and its deliverables.
3. **Access and distribution:** platforms meter retrieval, introduce buyers to sellers or manage delivery. A content-access payment does not by itself authorize training.

The categories increasingly overlap. Mercor markets operational-data licensing alongside expert work; Troveo has a business-data offering alongside video licensing. Both describe processing company records into assets for AI buyers. The opportunity for Sovereign Source therefore includes professional processes and documented know-how, provided the contributing organization can authorize their use. [Mercor Data](https://www.mercor.com/data/), [Troveo business data](https://www.troveo.ai/business-data).

Two distinct cash flows matter: **the lab pays the broker**, then **the broker pays a rights holder or worker**. Payment to a publisher does not establish payment to an author. Payment for expert hours does not establish an ongoing share of model revenue. SIP needs those beneficiary relationships recorded explicitly.

## Findings that affect a buying decision

### Supplier independence and security can change the market

Scale announced Meta's investment and an expanded commercial relationship on June 12, 2025. Bloomberg subsequently reported OpenAI's decision to wind down Scale work, quoting an OpenAI spokesperson. A historical OpenAI partnership should therefore not be presented as a current endorsement. [Scale announcement](https://scale.com/blog/scale-ai-announces-next-phase-of-company-evolution), [Bloomberg, June 18, 2025](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/openai-is-phasing-out-its-work-with-scale-ai-after-meta-deal).

WIRED reported in April 2026 that a Mercor security incident led Meta to pause work; OpenAI said its projects continued while it investigated. This dated report warrants current remediation evidence, not an assumption that every affected relationship remains paused. [WIRED investigation](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-pauses-work-with-mercor-after-data-breach-puts-ai-industry-secrets-at-risk/).

### Consolidation makes old broker lists misleading

Protege announced its acquisition of Calliope Networks on **December 18, 2024**; count them as one procurement group unless a current contract identifies separate counterparties. Cloudflare announced its acquisition of the UK-origin Human Native on **January 15, 2026**. These changes connect content sourcing with larger data and infrastructure businesses. [Protege acquisition announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/protege-acquires-calliope-networks-unlocking-premium-video-data-for-ai-training-302335162.html), [Cloudflare announcement](https://blog.cloudflare.com/human-native-joins-cloudflare/).

The shortlist emphasizes suppliers relevant to US labs. It does not certify US-only ownership, labor, data or processing. Human Native's UK origin is explicit. Sovereign Source's model-lineage constraint requires a separate audit of any models used to generate, translate or label purchased data, even when the broker is American.

### A revenue share can still leave contributors unidentified

Shutterstock's help page describes proportional allocation according to content and metadata included in a purchased dataset, and an average corporate royalty rate of 20% of its data-license revenue. That is a useful commercial precedent, but it is **not 20% of OpenAI revenue**, an individual guaranteed rate, or proof of per-output attribution. The same page says opting out concerns future datasets. [Contributor Fund mechanics](https://submit.shutterstock.com/help/en/articles/10594694-shutterstock-data-licensing-and-the-contributor-fund).

SIP's proposed 20% creator pool is a separate design with its own eligible-revenue definition and accepted agreements. The two percentages should not be described as equivalent. A broker agreement should identify the receiving syndicate, its authority, the beneficiary schedule and whether a broker payment offsets another agreed payment for the same use.

### Sample access and sales exclusivity deserve attention before onboarding

Defined.ai's supplier terms include company and dataset vetting, a separate licensing agreement, and permission to share submitted samples with prospective customers. Their non-exclusive framework does not promise minimum business. A syndicate should authorize sample exposure and confirm the eventual buyer grant before submitting valuable material. [Supplier terms, sections 2–3](https://defined.ai/supplier-program).

Troveo advertises repeated monetization and retained ownership. Establish separately whether a contract restricts which broker may represent a collection, whether buyers obtain exclusive uses, and whether earnings arise from each license sale or measured downstream consumption. These are distinct commercial questions; “recurring revenue” alone does not answer them. [Content-owner offering](https://www.troveo.ai/content-owners).

### Purchased human data can contain model-generated material

Surge's GSM8K account explains that OpenAI supplied generated starter problems, although workers often preferred writing problems from scratch. This does not diminish the human work; it illustrates why a supplier name or “expert-authored” label cannot certify the creation process of every record. [GSM8K creation account](https://surgehq.ai/blog/how-we-built-it-openais-gsm8k-dataset-of-8500-math-problems).

For a strict lineage policy, ask which teachers, judges, embedding models and rewriting tools touched the dataset and what role each played. Training text, labels and preference scores need separate provenance. A broker cannot clear a teacher's contractual restriction merely by reselling its outputs.

## Procurement plan for Sovereign Source

Start with one knowledge domain and two complementary orders: an authorized source collection and independently commissioned tasks evaluating its use. Invite **Created by Humans or Defined.ai** for the source collection, **Protege** where proprietary domain supply is essential, and **Surge or Turing** for original expert tasks. Evaluate **Mercor** for organizational workflows after reviewing current security evidence. Keep **Shutterstock and Troveo** for a subsequent multimodal phase. Human Native/Cloudflare remains an infrastructure conversation until its applicable product and contract are clear.

Send every bidder the same ten questions:

1. Who can authorize each asset, and what evidence supports the broker's mandate?
2. Which uses are permitted: retrieval, pretraining, fine-tuning, evaluation, synthetic generation, distillation, commercial serving and public weight release?
3. Which teacher and auxiliary models contributed, under which terms, and can their artifacts be excluded?
4. Which metadata identifies sources, authors, reviewers, versions and upstream influences?
5. What export includes the raw licensed assets, transformations, grants and provenance records?
6. Who receives money, what is the broker's share, and can the syndicate audit beneficiary allocation?
7. What price applies per accepted unit, including rejected records, replacements, updates and delivery costs?
8. What are the processing regions, subcontractors, retention periods and access boundaries?
9. What happens to delivered data, existing checkpoints and accrued payments after expiry, withdrawal or dispute?
10. What evidence demonstrates originality, useful coverage, deduplication and separation from the evaluation set?

Compare **cost per accepted, authorized, useful record**, rather than raw token count. Include licensing, cleaning, rights review, expert validation, secure delivery and ongoing obligations. Use a paid sample with a pre-agreed rejection policy before a large order. These are procurement criteria, not published vendor price quotes.

For implementation, the existing Python/Pydantic pipeline should validate asset and grant manifests; Prefect should orchestrate checks; PostgreSQL should retain permission and beneficiary records; Rust/Axum should enforce admitted uses and durable settlement events. Provider processing and syndicate accounting remain separate. This is a proposed integration with the [production plan](PRODUCTION_SYSTEM_PLAN.md), not an implemented service.

The commercial opening is to supply a **permissioned syndicate catalog with auditable contributions and payments**, then distribute selected collections through suitable brokers. Preserve the ability to verify grants and change distributors. Licensing a dataset should not require handing ownership of the attribution graph or beneficiary ledger to a broker.
