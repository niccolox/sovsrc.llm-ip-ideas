# Sovereign Source Syndicate Marketplace Terms

**Version 0.1 — Draft, October 10, 2026**

These terms provide a proposed contractual framework for a syndicate marketplace supporting the Sovereign Source Super Intellectual Property License. They take effect for a particular participant or transaction only when identified parties affirmatively accept them and the required schedule. They do not themselves establish a business entity, launch a marketplace, accept money or bind a contributor. Obtain qualified legal review before using them for real transactions.

## 1 Parties and acceptance

Each accepted marketplace participation agreement identifies the operator's legal identity and contact, the participant, the version of these terms, its effective date and the governing law and dispute forum for that agreement. A transaction additionally identifies every seller or authorized representative, the buyer, and any beneficiary whose consent is required.

Record the accepted terms and schedule as immutable versions with a digest, timestamp and reliable acceptance evidence. A seller's listing is an invitation unless it expressly states a binding offer. A posted proposal, invitation, draft order or AI-generated recommendation is not acceptance. Material amendments require the agreement's specified approvals; no silently updated website terms change an accepted order.

## 2 Marketplace purpose

The marketplace helps creators make their contributions discoverable, preserve credit and earn from agreed use. **SIP Creator Rewards** names its proposed contractual compensation program for works, ideas, implementations, research, datasets, models, services and documented influences. Permitted listing categories are:

| Category | What the listing supplies |
| --- | --- |
| Open material | Discovery, source access and its existing public license; any delivery or support charge is separately identified |
| Commission or bounty | A specified proposal, implementation, improvement, dataset preparation, evaluation or other deliverable under agreed acceptance criteria |
| Service | Hosting, support, curation, attribution review, research, integration or administration |
| Additional permission | Rights actually controlled by the seller that a public license has not already granted, including an expressly authorized alternative license |
| Voluntary revenue participation | An agreed share of identified transaction or product revenue in exchange for specified contributions or services |

Each listing states exactly what is supplied, the applicable rights basis, what remains openly available, and whether its intellectual relationships are claimed, reviewed or disputed. An idea or influence listing offers a proposal, service or agreed participation; it must not claim exclusive ownership of an abstract idea or a right to charge unrelated people for inspiration.

## 3 Separation from the open license

The [SS-SIP license](SS-SIP-0.1.md) can be used without an account, syndicate membership, marketplace fee or royalty. These terms apply only to accepted marketplace participation and transactions. Selling copies or services is allowed, but sellers must disclose rights the public license already supplies and cannot require buyers to surrender those rights.

No order or operator policy may convert a compliant open license into a revocable subscription, impose mandatory reporting on nonparticipants, or charge for every downstream use or generated token. An operator may charge agreed service fees, but may not present them as unavoidable license fees. A marketplace suspension affects marketplace services and listings, not independently granted open-license rights.

## 4 Syndicate authority

A syndicate must record an accepted charter, its members and the persons who can act for it. The charter defines its purpose, governance, admission/exit, voting, conflicts, fees, records access, appeals and dissolution. The syndicate's name alone does not establish its legal form or a representative's authority.

Every listed contribution requires an enrollment record. Every represented transaction requires a mandate identifying the granting party, relevant works or versions, permitted actions, term and limits. Participation does not assign ownership, license unlisted assets or authorize representation of a nonmember. A vote cannot expand a mandate or take an accrued entitlement from a beneficiary.

Default enrollment is nonexclusive. Exclusivity requires a specific grant over rights the participant actually controls, with its scope and term stated. A seller cannot grant exclusivity over rights already made available to the public under an irrevocable open license.

## 5 Listing and attribution duties

The seller must provide accurate rights and authority statements, applicable licenses, an Attribution Record, relevant provenance limits and any known dispute affecting the offer. Do not label an estimate as verified lineage or an adoption as legally established ownership.

A claim of an original contribution identifies what the participant added, such as an idea, analysis, implementation or improvement, and acknowledges the material it builds on. It does not replace rights review or make reused material the participant's own work. Licensed source material and agreed influence shares remain eligible under their respective agreements.

For paid use, the order specifies the required credit, placement, alias/privacy preferences, applicable source/manifest references and the treatment of documented influences. A supported influence may be acknowledged without payment. A payable influence share requires a named beneficiary, a defined contribution or relationship, an agreed split and acceptance by the parties whose economic terms it affects.

AI may propose matches, missing credit, valuations or summaries. A responsible reviewer must approve adopted records, and the actual parties must accept commercial terms. AI confidence, timestamp priority, popularity and graph position do not themselves establish ownership, representation or money owed.

The operator provides a missing-credit and correction process. Preserve meaningful uncertainty and a privacy-safe decision history. Required public credits use only material that can lawfully be disclosed; private evidence stays restricted. A record can be exported without revealing another tenant's source material or customer activity.

## 6 Order schedule

An executable order must contain all applicable information below. Fields marked not applicable must include a reason. A record missing a required field remains a proposal and cannot trigger payment, new permissions or production fulfillment.

| Schedule item | Required agreement |
| --- | --- |
| Parties and authority | Operator, buyer, seller(s), representatives, mandate versions and required beneficiary acceptances |
| Offer and deliverable | Listing/offer versions, contribution or artifact versions, service description, delivery method, timetable and objective acceptance criteria |
| Rights and source | Public license, separately granted rights, source location, permitted recipients/uses where applicable, third-party exclusions and surviving terms |
| Credits and influences | Attribution Record version, required credit view, review status, privacy handling and any agreed influence shares |
| Price or revenue basis | Currency, amount or exact formula, covered product/cohort, billable events, agreed deductions, applicable tax treatment and period |
| Distribution | Exact beneficiary shares totaling 100% of the stated distributable amount, disclosed fees, fee recipient and the fee calculation order |
| Adjustments | Minimums, offsets, refunds, chargebacks, cancellation, rejection, nonperformance, correction and hold rules |
| Payment | Due dates, payout threshold if any, payment method/provider, responsible payer and reconciliation procedure |
| Term and exit | Effective time, duration, withdrawal rules, surviving grants and accrued obligations |
| Resolution | Governing law, forum, notice contacts, review/appeal route and applicable remedies |
| Acceptance | Versioned terms digest and each required party's authentic acceptance evidence |

Future deliverables require an agreed rights grant for the delivered contribution. A commission does not silently assign every right in unrelated work. The parties must specify what happens if a deliverable is rejected, canceled or adopted only in part.

## 7 Rewards earnings and payouts

No economic obligation exists merely because a contribution is listed, credited, viewed or registered in a graph. The order identifies the economic event and its beneficiary schedule. An accepted order can create an enforceable contractual payment obligation subject to applicable law; a license file or AI assessment alone cannot.

An eligible creator is a contributor, rights holder or beneficiary who meets the accepted agreement's conditions. Qualified use means an evidenced, deduplicated event satisfying its earning rules, including an accepted adoption or delivery where specified. Rewards means the compensation calculated under those terms; that label does not make amounts already earned discretionary.

Statements distinguish provisional estimated earnings, posted earned balances, holds, adjustments and payouts. Each payout shows its actual status; initiating a transfer is not confirming payment. Eligibility reviews and appeals follow section 8, and departure preserves accrued obligations under section 9.

The default administration fee is zero unless an accepted schedule specifies otherwise. If the order deducts a fee before distribution, show the gross amount, exact fee, recipient and remaining amount. If the fee is instead a beneficiary share, include it within the 100% split and do not deduct it again. Preserve exact arithmetic, currency separation and reproducible rounding.

The project's proposed 20% contributor pool and 60/20/20 track allocation apply only when expressly selected in an accepted agreement. The schedule must define eligible revenue and the agreed allocation policy version. A marketplace transaction may instead use a fixed fee, bounty, minimum guarantee or another explicitly agreed formula.

For example, an agreed distributable amount of $2,000 with zero administration fee and a 60/25/15 split pays $1,200 to an idea contributor, $500 to an implementation contributor and $300 to a documented influence contributor. The allocation is a contract example, not a mandatory charge for use under the open license.

Resolve each payable event to one applicable agreement and split schedule per earning mechanism. Overlapping portfolios, retries and a second attribution path cannot create duplicate liabilities. Freeze versions at the agreement's specified eligibility time. Later corrections use linked adjustments; no one may silently edit a posted statement or redirect a beneficiary's balance.

Use an appropriately authorized payment provider for actual settlement. A bookkeeping hold is not a representation that funds are in legal escrow. The local pilot uses simulated payments and clearly synthetic parties.

## 8 Enforcement corrections and disputes

Participants must preserve contractually required credits and pay amounts due under accepted schedules. A claimant must identify the affected order, obligation, evidence and requested correction. The operator acknowledges receipt within a proposed five business days and targets an initial reasoned decision within 30 calendar days; the accepted participation agreement may set different deadlines.

Provide a reasonable correction opportunity, independent appeal and access to the relevant evidence through a confidentiality-preserving review. Reviewers must disclose conflicts and recuse themselves from their own claims. Hold only specifically contested sums or affected fulfillment, unless applicable law requires otherwise. Pay unrelated undisputed amounts on time.

The accepted order determines available remedies for nonperformance, missing credit and nonpayment; it may allow correction, replacement delivery, refund or contract termination. No automatic punitive damages or ownership transfer follows from a claim. The operator's administrative decision does not conclusively adjudicate legal title or bind a nonparty.

An accepted schedule may expressly name a beneficiary as entitled to enforce its own specified credit or payment obligation under applicable law. Mere mention in a notice or a pending influence claim does not confer that contractual right. Open-license claims follow the license's own scope and correction terms.

## 9 Departure removal and dissolution

Membership withdrawal and delisting end future representation or offers as specified by the accepted mandate and order. They do not confiscate accrued earnings, retroactively rewrite allocations or withdraw public open-license grants. Determine the status of unaccepted offers and pending orders explicitly; do not assume a delisting cancels an accepted transaction.

Dissolution identifies the responsible party for outstanding statements, payments, disputes and records. Participants can export their own applicable agreements, credit records, source references and accounting history in common formats. Portability does not authorize disclosure of private evidence or another participant's data.

## 10 Administration and limits

Operators must separate identity, rights review, listing administration and financial authority. A syndicate cannot set prices for nonparticipants, coordinate unrelated sellers' prices or claim special legal immunity through these terms. A listing is not a tradable investment interest in a person, idea or syndicate. Any different financial product would need its own authorized structure and review.

Applicable law and the accepted agreement control warranties, liability, taxes, consumer rights and dispute procedures. No participant may claim that the marketplace, this draft or an AI reviewer guarantees title, noninfringement, a return on investment or government approval. These terms supply no immunity or substitute for licenses the seller actually needs.

## 11 Version and independence

Accepted versions and orders remain available to their parties. A new version governs only where adopted through the required consent process. The operator may maintain reasonable future service rules but cannot use them to amend settled financial records or restrict independent public-license rights.

These terms are separate from SS-SIP-0.1. A rights holder may use the license without these terms; a marketplace may support other licenses while accurately identifying their obligations. Alternative operators may use and adapt this template without seeking permission, provided changed versions have a distinct name/version and are not presented as an unchanged approved agreement.
